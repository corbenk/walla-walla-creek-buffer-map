Walla Walla creek buffer · City Council public hearing

Wed Oct 14, 6:30 pm · City Hall, 15 N 3rd Ave

Agenda Item 6E. Three minutes per speaker, in person or by Zoom (us02web.zoom.us/j/84531798905, meeting ID 845 3179 8905; phone 253-215-8782). Testimony only that night; the adoption vote is scheduled Oct 28.

The ask, in four points

  1. Honor the Planning Commission's 7-0 recommendation of Sept 14: revise the draft before adopting it.
  2. Get a locally scoped best-available-science review.
  3. Replace the single 100-foot width with tiered buffers that reflect each creek's condition and fish use, and publish a parcel and housing-capacity analysis first.
  4. Protect existing homes in writing: repairs, additions and ADUs on the side away from the creek, and rebuilding after fire without losing grandfathered status in six months under existing code.

Email the Council

Email CityClerk@wallawallawa.gov, cc citycouncil@wallawallawa.gov, subject Item 6E. A line or two in your own words about your lot or your street, sent before the hearing so it is in the record. Or check your lot at creekchecker.com and use the drafted email. Speaking? Say your street and one point. Form letters count less than your own words.
