Washington · critical areas update

Klickitat County

Klickitat County and its cities face a June 30, 2027 state deadline to review their critical areas rules. A city under 25,000 people may adopt the county’s rules instead, and then does not do its own review. Here is what we know about its creek buffers.

DeadlineJune 30, 2027

Underway

Critical areas update began in 2022; in Nov 2024 the County withdrew its 2022 environmental determination and issued a new one on an updated draft. Current status unclear.

What you can do

  1. Look up your lot. Check any Washington address for mapped streams near it, the state fish and wildlife agency’s estimated streamside zone, and a what-if band.
  2. Read what the update is. The two-minute brief explains how state law, state agencies, your county and your city fit together.
  3. Find the draft and the next meeting. Start with the official links above. Drafts usually list the proposed stream buffer widths.
  4. Ask one clear question. For example: how would the proposed buffer apply to a lot like mine, and does it allow additions and rebuilding after a fire?
  5. Comment in your own words. A line or two about your own lot or street, sent by the comment deadline in the hearing notice, is usually included in the hearing record.
