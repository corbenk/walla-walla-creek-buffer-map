Today, proposed, possible future

Today · rules depend on location

Walla Walla’s listed creek buffers are generally 35 ft, with 50 ft on the listed Yellowhawk reach. Exceptions and shoreline rules apply. Unincorporated land follows County rules; College Place has its own code. The County’s tiered working GIS layer is not proof of an adopted rule.

Current City code · Table 6

Proposed · City of Walla Walla

The City draft proposes 100 ft per side plus a 15-ft building setback. It is not a blanket County rule. Mill Creek follows the Shoreline Master Program.

City draft · Table 6 & setback

Possible future · a what-if, not a forecast

The County’s planning charter includes a critical-areas update. That does not establish a future blanket 100-ft rule. If a wider buffer were adopted where you live, it could reduce space for new buildings, additions or a subdivision; the actual effect would depend on the adopted code and your site.

County project charter · page 5

Ask for local science, a parcel impact analysis and clear protections for existing homes before new rules are adopted.
