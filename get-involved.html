What residents are asking

The ask, in four points.

This is the request residents are making in testimony and written comments.

Read the four-point ask to the Council Use it in your own words

To the Walla Walla City Council

Honor the 7-0. Get the local science. Protect existing homes.

1. Honor the Planning Commission's 7-0 recommendation of Sept 14, 2026: revise the draft before adopting it.

2. Commission a locally scoped best-available-science review.

3. Replace the single 100-foot width with tiered buffers that reflect each creek's condition and fish use, and publish a parcel-level and housing-capacity analysis first.

4. Protect existing homes in writing: repairs, additions and ADUs on the side away from the creek, and rebuilding after fire without losing grandfathered status in six months.

No petition exists and no supporter count is claimed. The state deadline for this update is Dec 31, 2026; on Oct 14 Council asked staff for options by the end of November.
