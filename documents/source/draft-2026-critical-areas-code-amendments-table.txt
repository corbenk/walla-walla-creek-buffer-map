(RCW 90.74.010) Mitigation, in the following order of preference, is: 1. Avoiding the impact altogether by not taking a certain action or parts of an action; 2. Compensating for the impact to wetlands, critical aquifer recharge areas, and habitat or critical areas by replacing, enhancing, or providing substitute resources or environments; and 7. Monitoring the hazard or other required mitigation and taking remedial action when necessary.
