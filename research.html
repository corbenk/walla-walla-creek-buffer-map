How the numbers were computed

On 2026-10-10 we buffered the City’s own stream centerlines at 35, 50, 75, 100 and 150 ft in Washington State Plane South (EPSG:2927) and counted County tax parcels whose polygon touches the band and whose center lies inside city limits. Our 100-ft band and the City’s published polygon differ by 2.4 acres. Every band is measured from a mapped centerline; the draft measures from the ordinary high-water mark, so the regulated edge is farther out than drawn.

Why we link instead of host: the City’s license says its digital products “may not be reproduced or redistributed … without the express written authorization of City of Walla Walla,” and the County’s terms are similar. The map reads the public services live, and this page links them.
